Handbag Care & Storage

A little regular care keeps a leather bag supple, clean and in shape for decades. Here is a simple routine and how to store it well.

I

Everyday habits

Empty your bag regularly so heavy items do not stretch the leather or strain the handles. Keep pens capped and cosmetics in a pouch. Set the bag down on a clean surface, not the floor, and avoid hand creams or perfume just before handling light-coloured leather.

II

Cleaning gently

Dust the bag with a soft, dry cloth every week or two.

  • Wipe marks with a barely damp cloth and let it air-dry.
  • Use a cleaner made for leather, tested first on a hidden spot.
  • Never soak leather or use household cleaners.
III

Conditioning

Smooth leathers benefit from a leather conditioner a few times a year, applied thinly with a soft cloth and buffed off. It keeps the leather from drying and cracking. Skip conditioner on suede, nubuck and patent leather, which need their own products.

IV

Rain and stains

If your bag gets wet, blot off the water, stuff it with paper to hold its shape and let it dry naturally, away from radiators and direct sun. Deal with grease or ink stains promptly; for valuable bags, a specialist leather cleaner is safer than home remedies.

V

Storing well

Store bags stuffed with acid-free tissue paper so they keep their shape, inside a breathable cotton dust bag. Keep them upright on a shelf rather than hanging by the handles, and away from damp and heat. Wrap chains and metal hardware in tissue so they do not mark the leather.
