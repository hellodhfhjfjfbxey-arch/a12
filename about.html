About Valoria

Dear reader,

Valoria is an independent website about fine leather handbags. We write about the leathers bags are made from, the craft that goes into them and the care that keeps them beautiful.

We do not sell bags, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Our leather finder and size guide run in your browser and store nothing about you. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor, dated, and corrected when a reader spots an error.

You will find us at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
